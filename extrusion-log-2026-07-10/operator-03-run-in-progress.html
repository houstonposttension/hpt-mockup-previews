Design notes
Hero cycle counter: large numeral + progress bar shows 28/50. Most glanceable element.
Cycle log button: the biggest tap target on the screen — operators tap it after each cycle; 72px height exceeds shop-floor minimum (60px).
Cycle time warning: surfaces automatically when rolling avg > target. Supervisor also sees this alert on their real-time view.
Temperature: manual entry every 15 min; history shows last 4 readings with out-of-spec flag at 12:00 (186 °C vs 182 target).
Scrap counter: simple +/− buttons. Each tap auto-timestamps the scrap event.
Pause vs End: Pause keeps run open (operator break, material change); End → operator-04 final form.
