Record type is not a preference — it follows the count category (G.2). Full bundles and premade shapes replace on-hand, so they go on an Inventory Worksheet (exclusive). Drops are a delta into a management-allocated dom/intl bucket, so they go on an Inventory Adjustment. Cell/rack/bin detail stays HPT-side; NetSuite receives SKU totals against one location (G.5 — flips to per-cell if James finds Location records already configured).
If NetSuite returns partial success — say 3 of 10 lines wrote — HPT reverses the 3 and retries the full batch. Never partial. Auto-retry every 60 s for 15 minutes on network, 5xx and rate-limit failures. After the 15-minute window is exhausted it escalates to Teams #hpt-inventory-alerts, the count stays PENDING_RETRY, and you can retry manually or roll back. (G.6, revised 2026-07-09 — the CSV fallback was deprecated because a shadow data path is worse than a loud failure.)
