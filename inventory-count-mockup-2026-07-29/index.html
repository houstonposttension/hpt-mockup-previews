Why the approval chain looks like this
🔒 ISO 9001 §8.5.4 — Preservation & traceability

The process this replaces produces no audit trail. Counts are assembled by hand and keyed into NetSuite manually, so the reasoning behind every variance decision exists only in whoever produced it. HPT is pursuing ISO 9001 certification, and a quarterly inventory adjustment with no traceable approval chain is a §8.5.4 gap a surveillance audit finds.

Every count, edit, recount, write-off, approval and push is written as an immutable append-only event carrying event_id, event_type, actor_id, actor_role, timestamp_utc, sku_id, qty, location, reason_code and context_ref. Elevated events — recount, write-off, approval — add reasoning_text, previous_state and related_events. NetSuite pushes add netsuite_record_id, push_status and push_response_snapshot. (H.3)

Retention is 7 years (ISO 9001 + CFR), held append-only in Postgres and streamed to an object-locked archive for tamper-evident storage. The manager's single Approve & Send to NetSuite click is the explicit human approval moment the standard wants, and it locks the count — no silent edits after sign-off (G.2a, added 2026-07-09).

What's worth arguing about

1 · The rebar form is three different forms. Full bundles, drops and premade shapes share almost no fields. If a counter has to guess which tab they're on, this is where it breaks — and the tabs are the thing to react to first.

2 · The supervisor has five buttons on every row. Five is a lot of choice per discrepancy across 31 lines. If two of them are never used in practice, better to find that now than after Wave 4.

3 · The write-off codes are grouped, not just listed. The dropdown groups the eight codes as internal loss / vendor-recoverable / paperwork-only. That grouping is an interpretation of F.4's intent, not something the spec states outright.

4 · The push button asks you to type APPROVE. Deliberate friction on the one irreversible step. It may be one step too many for a manager doing this quarterly — the alternative is a plain confirm.

5 · Blind count is stated three times on the worker flow. Repetition is intentional — a counter who forgets it's blind starts second-guessing their own eyes. It may still be one banner too many.
