Supervisor + Lead Mobile · WIP (LEAN)

One role-scoped UI. Leads see their 3 rebar cells and their shift; supervisors see the whole plant, cross-shift, with Tendon/Hardware/Lumber as remote-manage panes. The physical scan drives the system — reroutes and repositioning are read from what workers actually do, not entered in a separate console.

WORK IN PROGRESS. Built: Q1–Q3 frames (reroute v1 + labeled future, supervisor/lead roster, dark-cell empty state) plus the new Approvals pillar — a reusable queue (split-load / scrap / VOID / price override) that partially answers Q5. Still HELD: self-service (Q5), repositioning (Q8), act-as-operator (Q6). Nothing pushed live yet. The request-submission side of approvals lives in the parallel Loader mockup.

12 Reposition HELD
"Move Alberto to M-3." Existing primitives could back it, but durable vs shift-scoped changes which one — and the whole interaction.
⏸
Awaiting Q8

Repositioning workers

Reassign a worker to a machine / cell from the floor.

Blocked on Q8 — durable vs shift-scoped: a durable role change (/api/portal-role) vs a temporary "this shift only" assignment (the ShiftOverrides 7-day-TTL primitive). One is a permanent edit, the other expires — different confirm copy, different undo, different audit.

13 Act-as-operator HELD
Supervisor substitutes in and scans as an operator/loader — reusing the LEAN loader components. The blocker is attribution, not layout.
⏸
Awaiting Q6

Act as operator / loader

Supervisor steps in and scans, reusing the LEAN loader kit + override scopes (void_scan, out_of_seq_op, reprint_tag, manual_capture_approve).

Blocked on Q6 — attribution: does the substitute scan credit the supervisor, the absent worker, or a distinct on-behalf-of actor? No impersonation primitive exists today — actor is the device's registered pseudonym. This decides whether the hourly grid credits the operator's row or opens a supervisor row.
