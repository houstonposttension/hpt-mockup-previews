Supervisor + Lead Mobile · WIP (LEAN)

One role-scoped UI. Leads see their 3 rebar cells and their shift; supervisors see the whole plant, cross-shift, with Tendon/Hardware/Lumber as remote-manage panes. The physical scan drives the system — reroutes and repositioning are read from what workers actually do, not entered in a separate console.

All 16 frames built. Situational awareness + reroute (Q1–Q3), the reusable Approvals pillar, 4 Self-service surfaces in two SSO-gated internal views (Q5), Act-as-operator (Q6 — credits the supervisor's own identity), and shift-scoped Repositioning (Q8 — every move expires end of shift). Order status uses the locked 6-stage lifecycle (Q4); the 9 target-less machines show a muted — no target (Q7). All 9 questions are answered — Q9 confirmed M-1..M-N was a stray placeholder (dropped). New: Workcell tile home + station drill-down (frames 17–18). Approvals' request-submission side lives in the parallel Loader mockup.

Reading this mockup. Frames 1–7 are the Q1–Q3 situational-awareness + reroute build; 8–10 are the Approvals pillar (reusable queue, approver side); 11–14 are the 4 Self-service surfaces, each in a Supervisor and an Office view (Q5); 15 is Act-as-operator (Q6 — credits the supervisor's own identity); 16 is shift-scoped Reposition (Q8 — writes ShiftOverrides, expires end of shift); 17–18 are the Workcell tile home (last-hour rate per station, needs-attention on top) and its station drill-down (adds shift-so-far + 8h sparkline + in-progress/queued tags). The approvals record is shared with the parallel Loader mockup (submission side) — see spec §5a. Numbers are illustrative. Every muted — no target (Q7) and "not scanning — wire station" marker is a real gap in the current system, surfaced on purpose rather than papered over. Reroute events follow the TAG_REROUTED_IMPLICIT schema in the design note (§5). Full design note lives in the private repo at docs/spec/supervisor-lead-mobile-design-2026-07-24.md.
