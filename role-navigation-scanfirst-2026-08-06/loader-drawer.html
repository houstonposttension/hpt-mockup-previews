4 / 7 · Loader — bottom drawer pulled up

The drawer is the answer to "what if a role needs a real, browsable task list?" It is collapsed by default (a 46 px handle), it opens on a pull or a tap, and critically it never covers the bar — the role tabs, cross-role pills and identity chip stay reachable at the top the whole time, and the scan surface is dimmed rather than replaced. Dismiss it and you are back scanning; you never navigated anywhere.

This is the loader's punch list — bin → category → missing tags, with a walk-to hint per category — and it ends in the close-out gate: a short truck cannot depart without a note and a photo.

Rules the drawer is carrying 1. Collapsed is the default state for every role — the handle costs 46 px and states its own count, so nothing is hidden.
2. The drawer never overlaps the bar, and it dims rather than unmounts the scan surface, so "open the list" is not a navigation.
3. Rows in it are the same .wrow as every other role's list, with the .val rule applied: percent, or an em-dash when there is no progress — never a severity. Severity moved out to a .pill/left border. That is a proposed answer to the audit's open question §9.3.
