Every control on this screen is named there: kit spec sheet, scan-to-consume strand, chair count entry, kit complete CTA; widgets today's kits, strand reserved for kit, kit progress; primary CTA "Scan strand into kit".

This is a kitting flow, not a tag-at-a-station flow. That is the substantive difference from Rebar and the Loader: the work unit is a kit that gets consumed into, not a tag that moves through work centres. The shared scanner cannot express it — which is exactly why the spec gives PT its own URL (/pt).
