My earlier supervisor page has been replaced, not patched. It pivoted on a flat roster of people with load percentages. House precedent applies — the persona mockup wins.

The tile metric is locked: last-hour lbs/hr, a trend arrow versus the prior hour, and a status colour against target — or against a 7-day average where no target exists, which is labelled as such. Shift-so-far is deliberately not on the tile; it lives in the drill-down. Stations with no target show "— no target" and no bar, never an invented one.

Frame A is the Lead home — scoped to their own cells and shift, groups expanded by default. Frame B is the Supervisor home — plant-wide and cross-shift, hotspot first, groups collapsed by default because there is more to scroll.

B · Supervisor — plant-wide, hotspot first, collapsed
What changed from my earlier supervisor page 1. The pivot. Person-roster → workcells and stations. The primary key is the place work happens, not the people. Tap any tile for the station drill-down.
2. Lead and Supervisor are separate. Different scope (own cells vs plant-wide), different default (expanded vs collapsed), different home content (rerouted-into-my-cells vs hotspot). My version had one supervisor state.
3. Presence is derived from scanning, never claimed as attendance. My earlier page showed "4 of 5 on shift" and "Off shift · no cover assigned" — an attendance signal that does not exist. There is no clock-in, and the locked spec records the decision that there is no software OOO handling. What the locked design actually does is safer and is kept here: the count comes from who has scanned this shift, so a worker with no scans reads "not in" rather than "absent". That is derivable from events.actor today. Nothing on this screen claims to know why someone isn't scanning.
4. Target-less stations are honest. Nine stations have no target. They render "— no target" with a grey dot and no bar. Where a 7-day average stands in for a target, the tile says so in italics rather than passing it off as a target.
5. Dark cells are a feature, not a blank. Three cells emit nothing at all. They say so, and the drill-down states exactly why and what the single fix is — see Station detail. The locked mockup calls this "the cheapest real improvement in the whole set."
Note on the bar: for a supervisor the role tabs are cells/stations rather than job functions, and the authority chip reads SUP. The consolidated bar still costs 38 px, which is the one thing rev 2 contributes here.
