Low-stock alerts are a named widget in §3.5. Bin-level roll-up is the supervisor half of the same signal.
B · low stock — the one thing PT's screen does not have
Grounding — and what has no data behind it Real today: Hardware is a live scan station (tag_type: hardware, scanning_yet: true) and one of the seven production WORKER_ROLES. It shows on the supervisor home as a real tile (1.1k lb/h) — with "— no target", because no throughput target has been set for it.
Not backed: there is no hardware kit entity, no bill-of-materials, no component↔kit link, and no stock-on-hand ledger anywhere in this system — hardware, lumber and consumables have no representation at all. Every quantity and every bin number on this screen is illustrative.
Overlap to settle before build: bin-level stock is arguably Inventory's data, and Inventory is a separate governed project (HPT-Inventory) with its own locked spec, its own location model and its own NetSuite boundary. The low-stock flag here should almost certainly read from that system rather than grow a second stock ledger inside WIP. Worth a decision before anyone builds it twice.
