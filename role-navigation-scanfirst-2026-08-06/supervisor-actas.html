15 / 16 · Supervisor — act as operator + reposition

These are pillars 2 and 3 of the locked design, and the first one directly overlaps the authority chip I put in the bar last night — so it needs settling rather than layering.

Act as operator does not mean acting on someone's behalf. The locked answer is that a supervisor who steps in and scans is credited to their own identity. There is no "on behalf of" prompt and no on_behalf_of field — the audit trail names the real person who did the work, and the hourly grid grows a supervisor row. That is simpler and more honest, and it needs no data-model change: the existing actor carries it.

Reposition is shift-scoped and cannot be made permanent. Every move writes a shift override with a 7-day TTL and shows an "expires end of shift" hint, so a careless move can't quietly become a reassignment.

The collision this settles — needs your call Last night I folded the authority swap into the identity chip: tap 0421 W to become 0421 SUP. The locked design already has a related-but-different flow, Act as operator, and the two are not the same thing:
• Authority swap changes what you can see and approve (worker view ↔ team view).
• Act as operator changes what you are doing (a supervisor covering a station and scanning), and explicitly does not change identity — the scan is credited to the supervisor.
Rendered here as two distinct things, with the chip carrying authority and Cover this station on the station detail entering act-as. If you want them merged into one control, say so — but merging risks implying impersonation, which the locked answer to Q6 deliberately rejected.
Known trap on reposition: every primary_shift write stamps a 7-day TTL that re-arms. That is the mechanism this feature depends on, and it is also the thing that silently clears worker shifts — worth confirming the shift-override path is the one that expires cleanly before this is built.
