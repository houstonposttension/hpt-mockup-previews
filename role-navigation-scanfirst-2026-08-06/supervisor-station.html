B · dark cell — no fabricated numbers
Locked answers this page carries Tile vs drill-down split. The tile shows last-hour rate only; shift-so-far appears here and nowhere else. That division is deliberate — the tile answers "is it running right now", the drill-down answers "how did we do".
Stall threshold is 4 hours on in-progress tags, and the stalled row is styled distinctly rather than just sorted to the top.
The dark cell is the pattern to copy. Three cells in the plant are dark for the same reason, and the diagnosis is specific enough to act on without a meeting. This is also the one place the supervisor surface names a concrete config fix.
