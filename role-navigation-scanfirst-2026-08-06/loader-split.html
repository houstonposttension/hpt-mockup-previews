The approval queue is a reusable primitive, not a loader feature Build it once as hpt-approvals with a typed request_kind (closeout_override first; scrap, void, price_override later) and every later approval flow is a new kind, not a new mechanism. The approver side lives on the supervisor persona surface — see Approvals.
Locked eligibility rules (2026-07-24), which my draft's two-row "needs your sign-off" list didn't carry: any lead or supervisor can approve any load — the queue is not cell-scoped — and self-approval is allowed: do not build a "cannot approve your own" guard. The audit still records both actor and approver_id; they may be the same id, and that is acceptable.
