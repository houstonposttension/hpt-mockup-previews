Restored from the canonical design, absent from last night's draft: the six-cell counts bar · the 4-part load header with its orders line · multi-loader presence + freshness ("Operator 2 · Operator 3 also loading · updated 3s ago", the 4-second poll) · the 409 conflict banner with attribution and the rolled-back optimistic mark · the three sub-orders as their own sections · the category filter (wrapped, never scrolled) · the bend-shape thumbnails · the row drawer with per-tag photo + hand-load · the "✓ N loaded" collapse · Start over.
One reconciliation call for your sign-off. Last night I proposed a bottom pull-up drawer for the punch list. The locked design already solves that with the In progress | Punch list toggle, which carries the outstanding count and the urgency-scaling rule (grey → amber at 80% → red on a short close-out). The toggle wins here — the punch list is a full second view of 14 rows across 5 categories, not a peek. The pull-up drawer stays on the rebar screen, where it is a peek.
