Why reject is heavier than approve Approve is one tap and the note is optional. Reject requires a reason from a fixed list, because the submitter has to know what to fix — same audit discipline as supervisor corrections elsewhere. The reason is stored on the shared record's decision_note.
C · reject — reason required, note optional
Contract this surface builds to The queue record is the shared source between the loader side and this one: request_kind · bin/run · shipped[] / carry[] · reason · actor (pseudonymised) · urgent · submitted_at · state · approver_id / decided_at.
Age thresholds are a build-time tuning knob; the mockup shows 2–9 min gold and 21 min red. Urgency comes from the submitter's urgent flag, which pins the request to the top and pulses on both the card and, at build, the nav badge.
Still unbuilt: there is no approvals store, no endpoints, and no CORRECT/VOID/SCRAP writer in production — those event types are read but never written. This whole pillar is designed, not built.
