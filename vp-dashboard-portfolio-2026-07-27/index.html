HPT Portfolio — concept mockup rev 3

One surface that answers "what is in flight, everywhere" and lets you push priority from inside it. Three views over the same data: Cards for the pulse and the levers, Timeline for when things land, Activity for what changed and who changed it. Red dots mark what needs you; the ⚙ sets how you get told. Every item is real work as of 2026-07-27.

Mockup only. Nothing is wired to a live system — no PR is approved, no priority is stored. Employee names are anonymized. Every date, effort estimate and hard deadline is a placeholder put there for you to correct — that's the fastest way to tell me what the real ones are.
