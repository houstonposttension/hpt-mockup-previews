4 / 5 · PT (Post-Tension) — shift material state, not per-piece capture

Redrawn 2026-08-08 after independent review. The first draft of this page drew per-roll scan/type capture, the same shape as Rebar and Hardware. That is the design uncovered-work-cells-2026-08-07.md ADR §3.3.1 formally rejected one day earlier, and this set should not have competed with it:

“Rejected alternative: per-piece heat entry. At 30–50 orders a shift with multiple pieces each, that is hundreds of entries per operator for information identical across all of them until a box runs out. It would be skipped — and skipped traceability is worse than none, because it looks complete.”

So PT captures material once per shift as an active material state, inherited by every order run under it, and re-captured only at a lot change. The identifier requirement is still this spec's; the mechanism below is the other spec's, which our §3.6 delegates to. Capture is by photo + vision extraction, not by scan or typing.

Identifier types updated 2026-08-08 (James). This screen captures three materials of two different types: Coil → Serial (which links to the heat), Anchor → Date Code, Wedge → Date Code. Earlier drafts showed all three as heats.

Coil is not a CertProcessor MaterialType — it is the physical form of Tendon, whose cert rule needs HeatNumber and SerialNumbers (_System/enforce_completeness_gate.py:59-62). The heat arrives by resolution from the serial, not by a second prompt on the floor (spec §2.4, §2.5, Q13).

Grounding — and what has no data behind it This page renders someone else's locked design, not a new one. Every control comes from uncovered-work-cells-2026-08-07.md §3.3: the three photo slots with extraction result and confidence per slot and a manual fallback (§7 screen inventory), the crew roster (operator required plus four optional roles), the always-visible active material-state banner, the order-tag QR as primary CTA with lot change always reachable as secondary, and the position marker — “I was on tag X of Y in order Z” (ADR §3.3.2, which also supplies frame C's “an order can carry two material states”).
Identifier types, James 2026-08-08: Coil → Serial (links to heat), Anchor and Wedge → Date Code. So this one screen captures two types, and only the coil serial can be verified today — Date Code is not a type the Cert Identifier Index models (spec §6.5.1), hence the unverifiable pill in frame B. From CertProcessor (cert completeness): "Tendon": {"required": [..., "HeatNumber", "SerialNumbers"]} (:59-62) — the heat arrives via §2.4 resolution, not a second prompt.
Not drawn, deliberately: remnant-cable provenance (ADR §3.3.3 — multi-hop source_cable_ref chains off a handwritten, unscannable tag) sits behind its own flag in that spec and needs its own frame.
Nothing here is backed by production data. There is no pt_material_state entity, no crew entity, no vision extraction and no order resolver. Both PT stations (PT Atlas, Hayes) are in the v8 registry but absent from config.SCAN_STATIONS (config.py:70), so they emit zero events, and the /pt page does not exist. PT capture cannot ship before PT scanning does — our spec §13 lists PT as a separate track that is not schedulable from this doc. Every heat, order number, count and timestamp on these frames is invented.
Open (our spec §12): Q10 — pt_material_state is shift-scoped and append-only, tag_cert_link is per-operation; one has to learn to read the other. Q11 — this screen captures anchor and wedge heats, the same physical items the Hardware cell kits. One capture or two?
