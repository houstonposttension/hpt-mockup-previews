Driver — pre-departure readiness (GA-WIP-218 · LEAN)

One question, answered on the phone before the driver leaves the yard: is my load actually finished loading? Drivers already carry the OptimoRoute app for route, navigation and proof-of-delivery — this view stops at the gate and hands off to OptimoRoute the moment the wheels roll. It is read-only: the driver writes nothing, and no new event types exist.

How a driver's screen works: the home screen is one load's detail — bin, progress, customer, site, ship date, ready-state. A truck carrying several bins shows each bin as a swipeable tab across the top (the strip is the whole-trip roll-up, kept inline); home is the first bin. The tab strip doubles as the driver's pick-list if OptimoRoute assignment isn't available yet. Data comes from the same /api/load-status + /api/loadlist.json the loader already uses.

Notes for review. This is a design mockup — data and names are illustrative. Real data comes from /api/load-status (per-bin loaded / fab_status / location) and /api/loadlist.json (today's loads), both already served and plant-IP-gated — kept as-is per the on-network decision. Two known gaps, shown here display-only, are documented in the design note: (G1) the "last tag scanned 2 min ago" freshness line needs the LOADED event timestamp surfaced into /api/load-status; (G2) the loader's name is deliberately not shown (worker names are tenant-only). The load↔driver assignment is pulled from OptimoRoute (search_orders · includeScheduleInformation), available only after the route is optimized — if that isn't ready pre-departure, the trip tab strip becomes a self-select pick-list over the same load data.
