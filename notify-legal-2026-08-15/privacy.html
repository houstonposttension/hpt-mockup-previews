Privacy Policy — HPT WIP Processor Operational Alerts

Effective date: 2026-08-15

This policy covers the SMS/text message alert program operated by Houston Post Tension, Inc. ("HPT," "we," "us") as part of the internal WIP Processor system.

What information we collect

To deliver operational alerts, we use an employee's name and mobile phone number, sourced from internal HR/payroll records (ADP) or information the employee provides directly. We do not collect additional personal information through this program.

How we use it

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver operational SMS alerts related to an employee's job duties — for example, system status notices, processing errors, or security-related notifications relevant to their role. This program is not used for marketing, sales, or promotional messaging of any kind.

How we share it

We do not sell or share phone numbers with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Phone numbers are shared only with our SMS delivery provider (Twilio) to the extent necessary to transmit the alert message itself.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on operational events and is not sent on a fixed schedule. Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier plan.

Opt-out

Employees may opt out of this alert program at any time by notifying their manager or the IT/operations team, or by replying STOP to any message received. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data retention

Phone numbers used for this program are retained consistent with standard employee records retention practices and are removed when no longer needed for the employee's role.
